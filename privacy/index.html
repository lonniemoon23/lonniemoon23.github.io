Your spending stays on your phone

Banked Privacy Policy. Last updated November 8, 2026.

What stays on your iPhone

Your purchases, the amounts you spend, store names and your daily number are stored only on your iPhone. They are never sent to us or anyone else.

Banked can't read your texts by itself. An automation you create in Apple's Shortcuts app sends matching bank alerts to Banked. From each alert, Banked keeps the amount, the store name and the alert's wording pattern, on your phone. The message itself is never saved, and you can delete the automation at any time.

Your birthday is checked once to confirm you're 18 or older and is never saved.

What we keep if you sign in

Signing in is optional. If you sign in with Apple, Google or email, we keep:

  • Your email address, and your name if Apple or Google shares it.
  • An account ID we create.
  • Your plan: the monthly amount you can spend, the day your month starts (and any change to it), your card payoff details if you added them, and the name of your bank.

We use this only so your plan follows you to a new phone. We never keep your purchases, the amounts you spend, store names or bank texts. We don't sell your data, use it for advertising, or track you across other apps or websites.

If you sign in with Apple, we also keep a token from Apple so we can disconnect Banked from your Apple ID when you delete your account. If Apple doesn't respond when you delete your account, we keep that token, encrypted, for up to 7 days solely to finish the disconnection, then delete it.

Subscriptions

Apple handles your subscription and payment. We never see your card. Apple tells the app whether your membership is active.

Where it's stored

Account data is stored with Supabase, our database provider, in the United States. Sign-in emails are sent through our email provider. They process it only on our behalf.

Deleting your account

In Banked, go to Settings, then Delete account. Your account and saved plan are deleted right away. You can choose to also erase everything on this iPhone. Deleting your account doesn't cancel your subscription; cancel it in Settings, then your name, then Subscriptions.

Email sign-ups that are never confirmed are deleted after 7 days.

Age

Banked is for people 18 and older.
