Banked support

Email lonniemoon01@gmail.com or message @trybanked on Instagram. In the app, Settings > Help copies a support code that helps us find what went wrong.

My number didn't move after a purchase

Banked updates when your bank texts you. Check that purchase texts are on in your bank's app, and that the Banked automation is still in the Shortcuts app under Automation. In Banked, open Settings and run Fix setup.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Banked.

How do I delete my account?

In Banked, open Settings and tap Delete account. It's deleted right away.

I got a new phone

Sign in on the new phone with the same Apple, Google or email account. Your plan comes back. Purchases stay on the old phone, so your new month starts fresh.
